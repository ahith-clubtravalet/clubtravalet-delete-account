ClubTravalet – Account Deletion Request

Last updated: October 13, 2026

ClubTravalet users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

How to request deletion

  1. Email: play.clubtravalet@gmail.com
  2. Subject: Account Deletion Request – ClubTravalet
  3. Include the email address or phone number linked to your account.
  4. We may verify your identity before processing your request.

What happens next?

We will review your request and delete or anonymise eligible account data in accordance with our policies and applicable law. Certain booking, transaction, tax, security, or dispute-related records may be retained where legally required.
